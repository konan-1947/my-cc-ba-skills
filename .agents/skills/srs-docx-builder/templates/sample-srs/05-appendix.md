## 5. Requirement Appendix

### 5.1 Business Rules

| ID | Rule Definition |
|---|---|
| BR-10 | AI-generated teaching materials must be based on supported KNTT textbook content prepared for EDUA. |
| BR-11 | Generated lesson plans must follow the structure required by MOET Official Dispatch No. 5512/BGDDT-GDTrH. |
